Published forecasts · later outcomes

Latest 50 receipts per input mode. Actual matured observations are separate from development scores. Pending, unavailable and failed evidence remain visible. Synthetic runs do not establish prospective accuracy.

Factor constellations

Every factor each stock has tried, sized by selections and development rounds beating the unchanged-price baseline, fading with time since its last trial. Lines show recorded derived inputs, co-selection and revisions. Opens a live map of the current history.

Failed research retains completed work. Continue reuses validated inputs and fitted models; code or input changes require a fresh study. Observation failures retain their evidence and can be retried by a new cycle.
Completed studies remain unchanged. A completed loop or a higher Sharpe does not demonstrate predictive advantage.
